1. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & CORE PHILOSOPHY
====================================================================================================
Traditional UAV monitoring systems rely on simple threshold exceedance alarms (e.g., if oil pressure
drops below 35 psi, flash a red light). By the time a static threshold triggers at 20,000 feet, 
bearing seizure or turbo failure is already imminent.

AERIS replaces passive alarms with an active Dual Digital Twin:
  A. THE ENGINE DIGITAL TWIN:
     Runs continuous first-principles thermodynamics in parallel with the real engine. It models
     air intake, turbocharger boost, 4-cylinder Boxer combustion, cylinder cooling convection,
     hydrodynamic oil film lubrication, and high-frequency structural vibration.
     Every sensor channel is compared against its physical expected value to compute a normalized
     residual drift:
         Residual (sigma) = (Measured_Sensor_Value - Expected_Twin_Value) / Sensor_Noise_StdDev

  B. THE MISSION DIGITAL TWIN:
     Models the entire flight plan (Ground -> Takeoff -> Climb -> Transit Cruise -> Loiter Patrol ->
     Descent -> Landing) through standard International Standard Atmosphere (ISA) altitude tables,
     calculating thermal fatigue, bearing wear, and oil degradation across time.

5. SECTION 3: GENERIC TWIN-ENGINE DIGITAL TWIN DETECTOR (CENTER PANEL)
====================================================================================================
LOCATION: Row 1, Center Column (spans 5 of 12 grid columns).

PURPOSE: Provides real-time cross-engine differential monitoring for any twin-engine MALE UAV.

WHY A GENERIC TWIN-ENGINE DETECTOR?
In twin-engine aircraft, ambient environmental variations (such as a 40°C temperature drop during
climb to 25,000 ft or turbulence load spikes) affect BOTH engines equally. 
A naive single-engine detector often triggers false alarms during climbs because temperatures drop
naturally. AERIS calculates the Cross-Engine Differential:
    Delta_MAP = MAP_Engine1 - MAP_Engine2
    Delta_EGT = EGT_Engine1 - EGT_Engine2
    Delta_CHT = CHT_Engine1 - CHT_Engine2
    Delta_OilPress = OilPress_Engine1 - OilPress_Engine2
By subtracting peer nacelle values, common atmospheric disturbances cancel out, isolating true
single-engine mechanical defects instantly.

DETAILED BREAKDOWN:
1. Nacelle Selector Tabs:
   - "DUAL NACELLE (TWIN)": Simultaneous side-by-side view.
   - "PORT (ENG 1)": Primary test engine view.
   - "STBD (ENG 2)": Peer benchmark engine view.
   - "TWIN Delta SYNC": Differential balance analyzer.
2. Dual-Nacelle Comparative Cards:
   - Left Card (PORT NACELLE): Shows active MAP, EGT, CHT, Oil Pressure, and Digital Twin Residual.
   - Center Card (TWIN Delta DIFFERENTIAL): Displays exact deltas (e.g., Delta EGT = +4.2°C).
     Prominently displays: "Common-Mode: CANCELED".
   - Right Card (STARBOARD NACELLE): Shows benchmark peer readings.
3. Powertrain Functional Flow (2x2 Grid):
   - Circuit 1: TURBOCHARGER & INTAKE (Boost Pressure, MAP, Residual sigma).
   - Circuit 2: 4-CYLINDER COMBUSTION CORE (Mean EGT, Engine RPM, Cylinder Balance).
   - Circuit 3: CYLINDER COOLING ENVELOPE (Mean CHT, Thermal Margin to 260°C limit, Cooling Health).
   - Circuit 4: LUBRICATION & OIL CIRCUIT (Oil Pressure, Oil Temperature, Lubrication Residual).
4. Per-Cylinder Boxer Matrix (Cylinders 1, 2, 3, 4):
   - Rotax 914 / Boxer layout: Left bank (Cylinders 1 & 3), Right bank (Cylinders 2 & 4).
   - Individual Exhaust Gas Temperature (EGT) bar and readout.
   - Individual Cylinder Head Temperature (CHT) bar and readout.
   - Live twin residual drift per cylinder (e.g., +0.2 sigma vs +2.8 sigma drift).

8. Structural Vibration: High-frequency accelerometer in g (normal < 0.1g, warning > 0.25g).

10. Bus Voltage: Avionics DC bus voltage (normal 28.0 - 29.5 V).

VIEW MODE TOGGLE BUTTONS:
- [MEASURED]: Displays the raw sensor reading directly from aircraft telemetry.
- [TWIN EXPECTED]: Displays what the physical digital twin calculates the value SHOULD be under
  the exact current altitude, ambient air density, and throttle position.
- [RESIDUAL (sigma)]: Displays the normalized deviation between Measured and Expected:
  * Green (< 1.5 sigma): Normal measurement noise.
  * Amber (1.5 - 3.0 sigma): Developing drift or minor wear.
  * Rose (> 3.0 sigma): Confirmed anomaly or failure mode.

8. SECTION 6: MULTI-HYPOTHESIS FAULT DIAGNOSIS & EXPLAINABILITY (ROW 3 LEFT)
====================================================================================================
LOCATION: Row 3, Left Column (spans 6 of 12 grid columns).

PURPOSE: Distinguishes between REAL MECHANICAL ENGINE FAULTS and FALSE SENSOR DRIFT.

WHY MULTI-HYPOTHESIS IS CRITICAL:
A single thermocouple reporting 780°C EGT could mean either:
  Hypothesis A: A real lean fuel burn or injector blockage (Mechanical emergency).
  Hypothesis B: A faulty thermocouple wire with an electrical bias (Sensor artifact).
Shutting down a UAV engine at 20,000 feet due to a broken sensor wire results in loss of aircraft.
AERIS runs cross-sensor physical correlation:
  If EGT rises BUT CHT and vibration do NOT correlate -> Isolated as SENSOR FAULT.
  If EGT rises AND adjacent CHT and oil temp rise -> Isolated as MECHANICAL COMBUSTION FAULT.

DETAILED BREAKDOWN:
1. Primary Diagnosis Card:
   - Displays the highest-likelihood fault hypothesis with Confidence % and Severity %.
   - Subsystem classification badge: SENSOR FAULT (Purple) vs MECHANICAL FAULT (Rose).
2. Decision Support Advisory:
   - Clear operational directive: "CONTINUE MISSION", "MONITOR RESIDUALS", or "RTB DIVERSION".
3. Explainability Evidence Chain:
   - Step-by-step physical causality audit trail showing how the AI reached its conclusion:
     * Step 1: Residual anomaly detection.
     * Step 2: Cross-engine twin comparison.
     * Step 3: Multi-sensor thermal and mechanical correlation.
     * Step 4: Final diagnostic isolation.
4. Alternative Hypotheses Evaluated:
   - Shows competing explanations with Bayesian posterior probabilities (e.g., Oil Seal Leak: 88%
     vs Oil Pressure Sensor Bias: 12%).

11. SECTION 9: FAULT INJECTION MATRIX & SENSOR ISOLATION TESTING
====================================================================================================
LOCATION: Modal window triggered by the "INJECT FAULT" button in the header.

PURPOSE: Allows evaluators to inject physical faults and sensor artifacts in real time to observe
how the digital twin and AI respond.

FAULT CATALOG:
1. Oil Pressure Loss (MECHANICAL):
   Simulates progressive seal leak. Causes oil pressure to drop and oil temperature to rise.
2. Cylinder 2 Cooling Degradation (MECHANICAL):
   Simulates cooling baffle loss. Causes Cylinder 2 CHT to rise while other cylinders remain cool.
3. Turbo Boost Deficiency (MECHANICAL):
   Simulates wastegate leak. Causes manifold pressure loss at high altitudes.
4. Crankshaft Bearing Wear (MECHANICAL):
   Simulates journal bearing wear. Elevates 2x engine RPM vibration harmonics.
5. Lean Mixture Cylinder 3 (MECHANICAL):
   Simulates injector restriction. Causes EGT3 to spike into lean-burn zone.
6. EGT3 Sensor Bias (+120°C) (SENSOR):
   Simulates thermocouple calibration shift. EGT3 rises, but CHT3 and oil temp do NOT rise.
   AI correctly diagnoses this as a SENSOR FAULT, not an engine fault.
7. Oil Pressure Sensor Bias (-15 psi) (SENSOR):
   Simulates pressure transducer drift. Engine vibration and temperature remain cool.
   AI isolates sensor fault without recommending unnecessary emergency landing.

12. MATHEMATICAL & PHYSICAL FORMULATIONS
====================================================================================================

1. AIR DENSITY & ISA ATMOSPHERE:
   P(h) = P_0 * (1 - L * h / T_0) ^ (g * M / (R * L))
   rho(h) = P(h) / (R_spec * T(h))
   Models the exact loss of volumetric efficiency and convective cooling at MALE UAV altitudes.

2. MANIFOLD PRESSURE (MAP):
   MAP = Ambient_Pressure + Turbo_Boost_Pressure - Throttle_Pressure_Drop

3. CYLINDER EXHAUST GAS TEMPERATURE (EGT):
   EGT_cyl = T_manifold + DeltaT_combustion(phi, timing) - Heat_Loss_Wall

4. NORMALIZED DIGITAL TWIN RESIDUAL:
   r_i = (y_sensor,i - y_twin,i) / sigma_sensor,i

5. MULTIVARIATE ANOMALY MAHALANOBIS DISTANCE:
   D_M(r) = sqrt( (r - mu)^T * Sigma^-1 * (r - mu) )
   Computes correlated anomalies across pressure, temperature, and vibration channels.

6. PROGNOSTIC REMAINING USEFUL LIFE (RUL):
   RUL = (Threshold_Limit - Current_Degradation) / (d(Degradation)/dt)
   where degradation integrates Arrhenius thermal stress:
       k_deg = A * exp(-E_a / (R * T_engine))
